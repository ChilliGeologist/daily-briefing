The thumb index

Built the way Niagara's alphabet works, with sections instead of letters.

  1. A small icon for each section sits quietly down the right edge, low enough to reach with your thumb: front page, Tasmania, Australia, globe, shield, server rack, controller, speech bubble.
  2. Put your thumb on them and slide. The icons under your thumb swell and bow out into a curve, the one you're on turns rust with its name beside it, and the paper jumps to that section as you go, with a small buzz at each one.
  3. Let go and the icons spring back into the edge.
  4. Or, while you're still holding, slide sideways off the edge onto a headline and let go on it to open that story.

Try it: hold your thumb on the right edge and slide slowly down to Gaming, then slide left onto a story and let go.

The strip only takes the thin right margin, and the page curl starts from a story, so the two don't clash.
